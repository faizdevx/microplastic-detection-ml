## Discrepancies to resolve

- Data counts: the response states 2,000 unique particle records. The image archive holds 2040 files that form 285 PVC and 315 Nylon near-duplicate groups (478 and 542 files are byte-identical copies), and the CSV rows have non-integer particle counts.
- Split protocol: the response describes partitioning by independent source image. The public notebook splits randomly, and its random folds put near-duplicates on both sides.
- Reported accuracy: 98.45 % +/- 0.53 % and 98.75 % hold-out come from a protocol and data set that the repository cannot reproduce. Group-aware cross-validation gives a fold mean of 0.976 and the group-level hold-out gives 0.987 on 155 images.
- Background and exposure: a classifier trained only on particle-free background discs reaches a balanced accuracy of 0.811, so part of any reported accuracy is not polymer information.
- Regression table: R2 0.78/0.84/0.84 and MAE 0.90/0.60/0.60 are computed on final_dataset.csv, whose origin is undocumented. Random forest and SVR have identical entries, and the repository's own table gives different values (0.807, 0.771 and 0.797 for Lasso, random forest and SVR).
- Model choice: SVR is stated as the final regressor; the repository's selection rule picks a different model on its table.
- Metric definitions: hold-out F1, precision and recall are quoted as ranges over classes; the repository reports macro-F1 and per-class values on a different hold-out set.
